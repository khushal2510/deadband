Process: gain 1, time constant 20 s, dead time 4 s, output limited to 0 to 100%. Lambda tuning uses Ti equal to the time constant and Kc = τ / (K(λ + θ)) with λ = 10 s. Ziegler–Nichols reaction-curve PI uses Kc = 0.9τ / (Kθ) and Ti = 3.33θ, which is lively by design.
