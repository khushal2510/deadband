Compressor types

Typical instrumentation and control practice for the three compressor families. The vendor P&ID, API datasheets and the client specification define the actual scope.

Anti-surge

Surge is flow reversal in a centrifugal compressor at low flow. The anti-surge controller opens the recycle valve to keep the operating point on the stable side of the surge control line. This is a schematic map for understanding, not a design tool.

A real controller runs a PI loop plus open-loop responses on a fast scan, using corrected (reduced) coordinates so the surge line follows gas properties, suction conditions and speed. Control line margins are set with the vendor.

Pump protection ideas

  • Minimum flow valve or recycle to prevent heating and vibration at low flow.
  • Trip on low suction level, low suction pressure or low flow with time delays.
  • Check valve on the discharge, with position confirmation where it is credited as a safeguard.
  • Standby pump auto-start on low discharge pressure or trip of the running pump.
  • Seal leak alarms that lead to controlled shutdown for toxic or flammable fluids.

Cavitation clues

  • Noise like gravel in the pump and unsteady discharge pressure.
  • Motor current and flow swinging with no change in demand.
  • Vibration at higher frequencies on the casing.
  • Confirm the NPSH margin (available above required) at the lowest suction level and highest temperature.

Machinery protection and vibration

API 670 defines monitoring systems for critical rotating machines. They are independent of process control, with their own sensors, power supply and trip logic.

Typical measurements

MeasurementSensorPurpose

Practical notes

  • Use trends and baselines. A machine that is stable at a given level is often healthier than one that is changing at a lower level.
  • Alarm (alert) comes first, trip (danger) is higher and often needs two channels to agree to avoid spurious shutdowns.
  • Start-up and coast-down inhibit the trip while the machine passes critical speeds.
  • Probe gap voltage, runout and cable length are part of the sensor calibration, not an afterthought.
  • Keep machinery protection hardware independent of the SIS and DCS, and send only status and trends to the DCS.
